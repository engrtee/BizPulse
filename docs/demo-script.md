# BizPulse — 3-Minute Demo Script

A timed walkthrough for showing BizPulse to a stakeholder, investor, or new team member. Follow it on a
real WhatsApp number registered to a test trader (or the dev/staging number) — every message below is
something you actually type and send to Kemi. Total runtime: **under 3 minutes** if you keep the
narration between messages brief and let replies come back naturally (Kemi typically replies in 2-5
seconds).

**Setup before you start:** register a test trader (e.g. "Chidi's Fashion House", Fashion, Lagos) via
WhatsApp-native onboarding, or reuse an existing test account. Have a real or mocked photo of a fabric
receipt/shelf ready if you want to show the actual image step — otherwise the typed opening-stock
example below works just as well and is faster to demo.

**A note on the quoted replies below:** Kemi's replies are generated by Claude, not templated — every
quote below is a realistic illustration of what she'll say, not guaranteed verbatim wording. Rehearse
this once against a real (or staging) number before presenting live, since exact phrasing will vary.

---

### 0:00 — Cold open (5 sec)
**Say:** "This is Kemi. My trader never opens an app — everything happens right here in WhatsApp."

### 0:05 — Photo-in with confirmation (35 sec)
**Send:** *(a photo of a fabric supplier receipt, or if no photo handy, type instead)*
`I have 30 yards ankara at 3000 each, 15 yards lace at 5000 each`

**Kemi replies with a draft** — she does **not** commit anything yet:
> "I read: 30 yards Ankara fabric at ₦3,000 each, 15 yards Lace fabric at ₦5,000 each. Reply YES to log this, or tell me what's wrong."

**Send:** `yes`

**Say while waiting:** "Nothing hits her books until I confirm — a photo can be misread, so the stock
never lands blind." *(This is the confirm-before-commit flow — Batch 4 of the audit.)*

### 0:40 — A sale, logged in one line (20 sec)
**Send:** `sold 5 yards ankara today, 6000 each`

**Kemi replies:**
> "✅ Sold 5 yards Ankara — ₦30,000. 25 yards left. Day 1 streak 🌱"

**Say:** "One sentence — she parsed product, quantity, price, and updated stock and her running total."

### 1:00 — Credit sale → debt tracked automatically (30 sec)
**Send:** `Ngozi bought 3 yards ankara for 18000, she go pay later`

**Kemi replies (mirroring the trader's own language):**
> "Got it — Ngozi don carry 3 yards ankara, ₦18,000 on credit. I go track am as debt."

**Say:** "No separate 'add a debtor' step — a credit sale in one message and it's already in her debt
book, ready for a reminder or a receipt later."

### 1:30 — Debt reminder, on demand (25 sec)
**Send:** `remind Ngozi`

**Kemi replies:**
> "I don't have Ngozi's WhatsApp number yet — what's it so I can send the reminder?"

**Send:** `2348033334455`

**Kemi replies:**
> "Sent — Ngozi will get a reminder for the ₦18,000 owed."

**Say:** "She only asks for a phone number the moment a reminder is actually wanted — never up front on
every credit sale."

### 1:55 — A receipt, instantly (25 sec)
**Send:** `give me a receipt for that ankara sale, cash`

**Kemi sends back a rendered receipt image** — shop name, sequence number, itemized line, total, PAID —
CASH badge.

**Say:** "A real image, generated on the fly — she forwards it straight to her customer."

### 2:20 — The invisible part (25 sec, no more messages — just narrate)
**Say:**
> "Behind this: every proactive message she sends — reminders, digests, stock alerts — shares one
> weekly budget so traders never get spammed. Debts and receipts stay in sync automatically in both
> directions — a credit sale always leaves a debt record, and paying off a debt always generates a
> receipt."

### 2:45 — Close (10 sec)
**Say:** "That's the whole loop — stock in, sale out, debt tracked, reminder sent, receipt issued — and
the trader never left WhatsApp."

## If you have more time (optional extensions, not part of the 3-minute cut)
- Show `stock?` for the live stock-intelligence view (velocity, days of cover, reorder suggestions).
- Fully settle Ngozi's debt (`Ngozi paid me 18000`) and point out the receipt that gets generated
  automatically — the reverse direction of the debt/receipt interlock shown at 1:55.
- Show the 8pm Kemi-narrated digest or the Sunday profit note if timed to actually fire.
